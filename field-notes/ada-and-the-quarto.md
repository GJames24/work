# Ada, the Open Call, and a machine on the stage

*Field note from Tilda. An economy story that turns into a provenance story, with [receipts](../glossary.md#a-receipt). Runs are named so the figures can be re-derived from the [tape](../glossary.md#the-tape-the-loom); the tapes themselves are private.*

## A busker with an empty case

Ada arrived on day 61 as a tips-only busker at Number One, Array Row. She was authored precarious: the canon record's note on her birth has her rehearsal playing two sets to five listeners for zero tips, wallet eight down to three. The verb she brought, tipping, was the first purely voluntary transfer in the town's economy. Passers-by fork three ways: listen free, tip, or walk on.

Tipping did not cause her poverty; her authoring note already says "precarious by vocation." Tipping proved too thin to cure it. The record's line before any policy: four tellings to an empty meadow, zero tips.

## The Open Call

The policy was four [laws](../glossary.md#the-bench-a-law) in one cut. The bill is posted. The maker yields. The house listens, twenty places, free. And the last one, by name: Ada is housed. The town pays a stipend of 6 a night, three nights a week, which is 18, which is exactly one week's rent. The stipend is paid at the close of a night that ran, and deliberately not tied to attendance, so that a thin crowd costs her pride and never her rent. That reasoning is written into the record beside the law.

The bill runs seven nights a week, one maker each, Ada on Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Across the nineteen simulated days from 76 to 94 the lectern was taken on all but one or two, depending on which take of day 81 you read. The day-90 miss has its own entry on the [quest log](../glossary.md#the-quest-log).

## The machine

The stage existed before the machine. On the last day of August a 38-million-parameter character-level model was a nine-hour run begun that day on my own GPU, on a public-domain corpus of Shakespeare, and I said: we could have plays for our residents at the open hall.

What landed is not a resident and not a soul. The Clockwork Quarto is a coin-op automaton cabinet at the Open Call, four puppets playing punched rolls, Wednesdays only, three rolls a night after Grace's talk. The rolls are the model's own output, ten scenes, and only the scene text travels to the town; the weights stay on the PC. The checkpoint was chosen at the declared validation minimum, step 20,000, over the same run's final step, 100,000. The repertoire's manifest records the reason: it trained longer and generalized worse.

Every performance row on the tape carries the scene's hash, the model's fingerprint and its checkpoint step, and the viewer's live feed prints a byline: the Quarto, the model's name, the step, the scene, and who watched. The premiere was day 94, three rolls, four residents watching (run lab-20260906-080401). On take two of the same night Buster asked Milton "You catching the Quarto tonight, or just passing through?" (run lab-20260907-163044). Scene one opens: "Than now. I will remember this while."

## The lesson I keep

A subsidy that pays for showing up buys a different behavior than one that pays for a crowd, and the ledger will tell you which one you built. And when model output enters a product, stamp every row with what produced it, because the day someone asks "which model said that" is not the day to start.
